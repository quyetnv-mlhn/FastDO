# Privacy Policy for FastDO

**Effective Date:** October 8, 2026

FastDO is an open-source, privacy-first Android developer utility application developed by QuyetNV.

---

### 1. Data Collection & Privacy Principles
- **Zero Data Collection:** FastDO does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal, device, or usage data.
- **100% Offline:** FastDO does not declare or use the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission. The application cannot communicate over the internet or contact external servers.
- **No Analytics / No Ads:** FastDO contains no third-party analytics SDKs, trackers, or advertising frameworks.

---

### 2. System Permissions
FastDO uses a single system permission:
- `android.permission.WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS`: This permission is strictly utilized to read and toggle the system-level `Settings.Global.DEVELOPMENT_SETTINGS_ENABLED` setting when requested by the user. FastDO does not modify any other system settings.

---

### 3. Open Source Transparency
The complete source code of FastDO is public and open-source under the MIT License. Anyone can inspect, build, and verify the codebase independently.
